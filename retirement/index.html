Retirement Corpus Calculator

Corpus you need at retirement and the SIP to build it.

Inputs

  • Current age
  • Retirement age
  • Plan until age
  • Monthly expenses today
  • Inflation (% per year)
  • Return before retirement (%)
  • Return after retirement (%)
  • Savings already invested

How it is calculated

Expenses are inflated to retirement, the corpus is the present value of withdrawals that grow with inflation, and the SIP is solved against the shortfall.
